Why Regime-Aware?

A single static bias correction fails during the monsoon. Physical models over-predict light drizzle during Break Monsoons but under-predict intense convective cells during Bay Depressions. VARSHAAI adapts its calibration to the active regime.

Honest AI & Transparency

Zero black-box hallucinations. All metrics (RMSE, MAE, CSI, POD, FAR) are mathematically calculated from the 10,000 prototype records. Unmeasured metrics are transparently labeled rather than fabricated.

What do these verification metrics mean? (Click to expand)

RMSE (Root Mean Square Error): Measures typical error magnitude in mm. Lower is better.

MAE (Mean Absolute Error): Average absolute difference between forecast and observation. Lower is better.

CSI (Critical Success Index / Threat Score): Evaluates heavy rain hit rate taking misses and false alarms into account. Range 0 to 1; higher is better.

POD (Probability of Detection): Proportion of actual heavy rain events correctly predicted. Higher is better.

FAR (False Alarm Ratio): Proportion of predicted heavy rain events that failed to materialize. Lower is better.

ETS (Equitable Threat Score): Skill relative to random chance forecast.

Scientific Methodology & Justification

The scientific rationale behind regime-aware AI post-processing for SIH 26080.

1. Why Regime-Aware?

Atmospheric convective dynamics vary radically across weather regimes. Under a Break Monsoon, thermodynamic lapse rates are capped by subsidence, while during a Monsoon Low, deep moisture convergence leads to massive convective towers. A single global regression model applies uniform attenuation or amplification, exacerbating errors in opposing regimes. Regime-awareness ensures that the statistical post-processor applies calibrated transfer functions suited to the physics of the prevailing atmospheric state.

2. Why Post-Processing Rather than Replacing NWP?

Physical Numerical Weather Prediction models (such as NCMRWF NEPS-G) solve the non-hydrostatic Navier-Stokes primitive equations of atmospheric motion. They provide irreplaceable conservation of mass, momentum, and thermodynamic energy. Machine learning is best utilized to post-process systematic parameterization errors (sub-grid convective parameterization, terrain smoothing, and boundary-layer fluxes) rather than attempting to re-invent atmospheric physics from scratch.

3. Why Rigorous Verification?

An AI modification to a numerical forecast is meaningless unless it demonstrably improves forecast skill against observations. VARSHAAI embeds standard meteorological verification metrics (RMSE, MAE, CSI, POD, FAR, ETS) to provide technical evaluators and meteorologists with verifiable evidence of model value.
